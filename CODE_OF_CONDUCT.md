# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to creating and maintaining a respectful, inclusive community where all contributors feel welcome. We value:

- **Respect**: Treating everyone with courtesy and consideration
- **Inclusion**: Welcoming people of all backgrounds and experience levels
- **Collaboration**: Working together toward shared goals
- **Learning**: Supporting each other's growth and development

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional in all interactions
- Listen actively to different perspectives
- Provide constructive feedback
- Ask questions rather than making assumptions
- Acknowledge and credit the work of others
- Focus criticism on ideas, not individuals
- Help create a safe space for all participants

## Unacceptable Behavior

We do not tolerate:
- Harassment, intimidation, or bullying
- Discrimination based on identity, background, or beliefs
- Offensive language or personal attacks
- Trolling, spam, or disruptive behavior
- Sharing others' private information without consent
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome advances
- Any form of violence or threats

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. **Document** the incident (date, time, who was involved, what happened)
2. **Report** to the maintainers at conduct@oldflick.com
3. **Stay safe** - your well-being is the priority

All reports will be:
- Taken seriously
- Investigated promptly and fairly
- Handled confidentially
- Protected from retaliation

## Enforcement

Maintainers will respond to reports by:
1. Investigating the incident thoroughly
2. Determining appropriate action
3. Communicating decisions and next steps
4. Following up to ensure resolution

Consequences may include:
- Warning the individual
- Temporary suspension
- Permanent removal from the community
- Escalation to authorities if necessary

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- GitHub issues and pull requests
- Project discussions and forums
- Mailing lists and chat channels
- In-person events and conferences
- Any space associated with the OldFlick project

## Acknowledgments

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Questions?

If you have questions about this Code of Conduct:
- Review the full text above
- Email conduct@oldflick.com
- Open a discussion on GitHub

## Our Commitment to You

We are committed to:
- Enforcing this code fairly and consistently
- Listening to community feedback
- Continuously improving our community
- Protecting all contributors

Together, we can build a positive, productive community where everyone can do their best work.
